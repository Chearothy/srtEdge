    Generate

    Browser voices play through your speakers. Keep “Record” ticked to get a download when it finishes, or switch to “AI voice” for a clean, exact file.

    When you press Start, your browser asks what to share. Choose This tab and turn on Share tab audio. Keep the volume on and let it play to the end. The recording is real-time.

    AI voice: each line is generated with your selected Gemini key (API settings tab), placed on the timeline at its timestamp, and mixed into one WAV file. Browser voice plays live and can be recorded. Styles are simulated with pitch, speed and volume.

    API settings

    Add as many AIs or API keys as you want. Tick the one you want to use, then press Save.

    Export saves a .json file with your keys in plain text, so keep it private. Import skips AIs already in your list.

    Which AI should I buy?

    Pick what matters most. Clipdesk ranks tools using your clip length from the Prompts tab. Prices are starting prices from recent reviews, so check the site before you pay.

    AI tools

    The list order is the fallback order: if a tool can’t make the chosen length or has no credits left, Clipdesk tries the next one. Turn on the tools you use. Add an API endpoint and key to generate from this page; leave them empty and Clipdesk copies your prompt and opens the tool’s site.
